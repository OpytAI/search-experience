Why collections exist

Documentation and blog are not the same mental pile. Collections keep that honesty in crawl and UI without teaching the host to rank.

The product pressure

Integrators ask for “search everything” and “but not like one bag of URLs.” Those are both true. Flat indexes force people into brittle query tricks; overbuilt host rankers invent policy that should live next to the index.

The cut we chose

A collection is seeds + presentation + optional prefix. The guest still owns generations and fusion. This docs site is itself the demo: docs: and blog: are real scopes over the pages you are reading.

What we still refuse

Host-side section boosts, security cosplay with unenforced prefixes, and treating the service worker as a crawler. Configure seeds as if they matter.
