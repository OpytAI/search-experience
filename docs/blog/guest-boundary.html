Living with a guest boundary

Putting policy in a Wasm guest is a product choice with costs. Here is what it buys and what it demands of integrators.

What gets easier

One authority for crawl and rank. Reproducible generations. A clear answer to “where do I debug relevance?”—not “which of three TypeScript files last touched score?”

What gets harder

You carry kernel and image bytes. Snapshots invalidate when those digests move. Debugging means understanding serviceCall and OPFS, not only Network waterfalls.

Why we still pick it

Static hosting plus honest client-side search is the job. A guest is how we keep that job from rotting into an ad-hoc browser database with accidental ranking.
